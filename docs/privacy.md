# Privacy

## Philosophy

Spendly is designed around privacy-first principles.

Financial information is highly sensitive and should remain under user control.

---

## Local Data Storage

User data is stored locally on the device.

Examples:

- Expenses
- Income
- Budgets
- Analytics

---

## Biometric Authentication

Spendly supports:

- Fingerprint authentication
- Face authentication
- Device PIN fallback

The app can be locked whenever it enters the background.

---

## Hidden Amounts

Users can hide financial amounts from the interface.

Useful when:

- Sharing screens
- Taking screenshots
- Using the app in public

---

## Data Ownership

Users remain the owners of their data.

Spendly is designed to minimize reliance on cloud services.

---

## Future Improvements

Planned:

- Encrypted backups
- Secure vault
- Export protection
